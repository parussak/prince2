Chapter 8: Quality

Purpose and SnakeBite example

The quality practice defines the characteristics products must have and how they will be checked. Maya says SnakeBite should run smoothly on a phone. Zosia turns that expectation into measurable acceptance criteria and quality criteria in product descriptions. Kamil runs frame rate and unit tests as quality control. Independent quality assurance examines whether the agreed methods are suitable and used consistently. A passing automated test alone does not constitute formal product acceptance.

The project product description captures the overall product and its acceptance criteria; an individual product description sets criteria and quality methods for a component. The quality management approach defines the project’s quality arrangements. The quality register records planned checks and results. The product register tracks product status and approvals. A review by someone independent of the producer strengthens confidence when required.

Key terms, responsibilities and management products

Quality planning: agree user quality expectations, acceptance criteria, standards, methods, responsibilities and tolerances. Quality control: inspect or test products and record results. Quality assurance: independently confirm that quality arrangements provide confidence. Project assurance represents the board’s interests and is a different governance role. Service level agreements may contribute quality requirements, but they are not the whole quality plan.

What are user quality expectations?

A high level description of what users expect from the finished project product.

What is a quality tolerance?

Permitted variation around a quality criterion, where one has been agreed.

Who agrees acceptance criteria?

The user and business interests approve criteria through the project’s agreed authority.

Quality assurance versus project assurance?

Quality assurance reviews quality arrangements independently; project assurance checks the project for the board.

Quality register versus product register?

The first records checks and their results; the second tracks product status and approval.

Additional textbook-aligned explanation

From requirements to acceptance

Quality planning determines expectations, acceptance criteria, quality specifications and the methods and responsibilities for verification. Each product description records its quality criteria, methods and responsibilities. Quality control carries out the specified checks and records results in the quality register. The product register tracks the products and their status, including approval and acceptance. A passed automated test supports a decision; it is not automatically the recipient’s acceptance of the project product.

Quality assurance and project assurance

Quality assurance is independent of the project management team and checks that the organization’s quality management system is adequate and followed. Project assurance acts on behalf of the project board and checks that the project is being conducted properly from business, user and supplier perspectives. Neither role substitutes for carrying out product quality control. A review should involve people sufficiently independent of the product’s author and competent to judge its specified criteria.
