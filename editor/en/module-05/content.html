SnakeBite Scenario: Business Case

Why do we use the Business Case practice?

Purpose of the practice:create mechanisms for assessing whether the project is and remains desirable, feasible and capable of achieving the expected results and benefits. This assessment supports decisions about further investment in the project.

It's an ongoing way of making decisions, rather than writing a document once for budget approval. If validity disappears, the project should be changed or stopped.

Validity testReview questionSnakeBite
Desirable - desirableDoes the balance of benefits, costs and risks justify the investment?Does the expected value of the game justify the investment and market risk?
Feasible - viableCan we supply the required products?Can the team and suppliers deliver the game and infrastructure under the agreed conditions?
Achievable - achievableCan the use of the products lead to the planned results and benefits?Even a ready-made game will not bring the expected results if we do not reach the players or they do not use it.

Exam trap:the feasibility of a product does not prove the feasibility of obtaining benefits. A project may be technically feasible and yet not make business sense.

On what basis do we document the validity of the project?

Business case as a documentcompares estimated costs with expected benefits, taking into account the associated risks. It also determines how and when the benefits can be measured. The value of a project is more than the lowest cost or revenue alone.

ComponentWhat do we agree on?
Business reasons and goalsWhy is change needed and how will it contribute to the organization's strategy?
Business optionsWe compare: change nothing, do the minimum and do more than the minimum. No change is the baseline. A mandatory project also requires consideration of implementation variants.
Benefits and disadvantagesWe define measurable improvement and deterioration relative to the baseline situation, measurement deadlines and benefit tolerances.
Costs and financingWe take into account the implementation of the project, subsequent operation and maintenance, and sources of financing.
TimeWe separate the project duration from the period of obtaining benefits.
Investment and risk assessmentWe compare the value of benefits and disadvantages with inputs and additional operating costs. We disclose assumptions, key threats and opportunities, and responses to them.
SustainabilityWe set specific goals and tolerances related to the organization's goals.
Clarification of earlier history
Artur called the difference PLN 1.3 million in revenues and PLN 500,000. PLN of expenditures "net profit". This is a simplification:800 thousand PLN is not automatically net profit. You need to take into account, among others: maintenance, commissions, marketing and taxes. The increase in the cost of servers must be expensed; the same amount may not be deducted again as a disadvantage.

Business case in the project life cycle

MomentWhat's going on?Decision or responsibility
Mandate/order to prepare the projectBusiness sets expectations and limits. This information provides a starting point for the initial justification.The business level provides the mandate.
Starting the project - SUIt's risingpreliminary Business Case(outline business case), included in the Project Brief. Justifies the investment in initiation; the data does not yet have to be sufficient to approve the entire project.The council in DP allows initiation.
Project Initiation - IPThe initial justification develops infull Business Case(full business case). Detailed plans and analyzes provide a stronger basis for decisions.The DP council approves the implementation of the project.
Implementation and boundaries of stagesWe check the impact of progress, risks and issues. At the end of the stage, we update execution, costs, benefits achieved and forecasts. We verify the validity before the next stage and when assessing the Exception Plan.The project manager updates the information; The Project Board assesses the viability of further investment.
Closing the project - CPWe assess execution and the likelihood of achieving other outcomes and benefits. We establish further measurements and responsibilities.Closure does not mean that all benefits have already occurred.
After the projectBenefit reviews check the actual effects of using products against assumptions.Business ensures business continuity. The Primary User is responsible for confirming the expected benefits.

Four activities that must be distinguished

  • Develop:Analyze your options and gather the information you need to evaluate your investment.
  • Check - check:assess whether the project is still worth implementing.
  • Keep up to date - maintain:update the rationale with performance data and the latest forecasts; keep previous versions for comparison.
  • Confirm - confirm:check the achievement or prospect of achieving benefits. Benefits may emerge during the project, although many will only occur after closure.

Results, capacity, results and benefits – the complete chain

In this moduleresult/product means output, aresult means outcome. The colloquial use of the word "result" in both senses blurs a key difference.

ConceptMeaningSnakeBite example
Result/product – outputA tangible or intangible product of work; in PRINCE2, a specialist product enabling change.Tested game, payment system, analytics and user manuals.
CapabilityA complete set of products needed to achieve the result.PixelBite has a complete solution that allows you to share the game, serve players and settle payments.
Result - outcomeA change in behavior or actual conditions resulting from implementing a change.Players regularly use the game and make purchases, and the company supports this new sales channel.
Benefit - benefitMeasurable improvement resulting from a result that is considered beneficial by the organization and supports its goals.Margin increase by a fixed amount relative to the no-project scenario, measured over a fixed period.
Disbenefit - dis-benefitMeasurable deterioration resulting from a result, assessed negatively and hindering the achievement of business goals.Confirmed increase in average waiting time for customers of other games when the shared support department also supports SnakeBite.
Business objective - business objectiveA measurable effect showing the progress of strategy implementation.Increasing the profitability of the game portfolio; SnakeBite benefits are designed to make a measurable contribution to this goal.

Relation:products create the capacity for change. Its actual implementation and use leads to results. Outcomes can result in benefits and disbenefits, and the benefits support business goals. The relationship does not have to be one-to-one: several products can work together to enable one result.

There is no automatism:the reception of a game does not mean that players will accept it. Promotion, changing the way of working, support training and removing barriers to use may be needed. Disadvantage is not the same as uncertain risk - we assess the uncertainty of a negative event as a threat.

An approach to sustainable development management

Sustainability management approachis part of the Project Initiation Documentation (PID / PID). Identifies the actions, reviews and controls needed to achieve the project's sustainability goals.

Division of document roles:The Business Case defines the sustainability objectives and tolerances and their relevance to validity. The approach describes how we will manage them and how we will check achievement. Goals should support the environmental, social and governance – ESG – goals of the organization.

Element of approachWhat are we describing?SnakeBite teaching example
RangeWhich objectives are covered by management and measurement?The energy of the infrastructure covered by the solution and the availability of the game.
MeasurementThe methods, terms and baselines against which we evaluate goals.Energy for 1000 sessions at comparable load; baseline measurement, pre-release test and post-deployment review.
ResponsibilityWho performs the activities and who is accountable for measuring the achievement of goals?In the project arrangements, Kamil provides infrastructure data, and the indicated owner of the measurement confirms the results.
ResourcesTime, competences and financing of activities.Infrastructure specialist's time and accessibility testing budget.
Tools and techniquesHow do we obtain and analyze data?Infrastructure monitoring, comparative load testing and availability audit.
Standards and referencesApplicable requirements and related documents.Company ESG policy, agreed accessibility requirements, Business Case and test plans.

Further story:Maya no longer accepts the generic term "green servers". Zosia writes down an example goal: at least 15% less energy per 1000 sessions compared to the baseline measurement, with the same load profile and service quality. This is a didactic assumption, not a textbook requirement.

Kamil plans optimization and benchmarking. Zosia sets the review date, data source and owner of the measurement after the project closes. If the forecast falls outside the agreed tolerance of the appropriate level, the matter is escalated. Merely choosing a supplier that advertises itself as ecological does not prove that the goal has been achieved.

Don't confuse the approaches:the benefits management approach organizes activities leading to results and benefits reviews; The sustainability management approach organizes the activities, measurement and control of sustainability goals. They may use the same data, but have separate purposes.
