PRINCE2 7 identifies five integrated elements: principles, people, practices, processes, and the project context. The four tiles below introduce principles, people, practices and processes. The project context is the fifth element.

Source: Managing Successful Projects with PRINCE2, chapter 2.

Zosia (Project Manager) sits in a glass conference room PixelBite Games. Opposite her Artur (Sponsor / Executive), tapping a pen on the table.

"Zosia, the budget for the SnakeBite retro game is PLN 500,000, and we have exactly six months. I am interested in the actual profits from microtransactions. If it becomes clear during the project that the 8-bit gaming trend has faded, we will shut the project down immediately!" – said Artur.

Maya (Senior User) interjected: "We surveyed players – they want a return to nostalgia gaming! Moreover, management requires us to reduce the servers’ carbon footprint. Our game must meet stringent environmental standards (Sustainability)!".

📐 Five integrated elements of PRINCE2 7

PRINCE2 is a project management method. PRINCE2 7 has five integrated elements: principles, people, practices, processes, and the project context. Flip the tiles to explore four of them; apply all of them to the specific project context.

⚖️
1. Principles (Principles)
Seven guiding obligations. All seven must be applied for a project to be managed using PRINCE2.

🎓 Foundation Exam Knowledge
Official definitions, documentation, and a knowledge test.
Project
A temporary organizational structure created to deliver one or more business products according to a defined Business Case.
Sustainability (Sustainability in Projects)
A key aspect in the 7th Edition. It ensures that the project considers environmental, social, and economic objectives (ESG) at every stage of product delivery.

📄 Management Products – Introduction

Management Document Type Description and Role in the Project
Project Brief
(Project Brief)		 Baseline Created in SU. It provides the starting point for deciding whether to begin project initiation.
Project Initiation Documentation
(PID - Project Initiation Documentation)		 Baseline Created in IP. The management baseline containing plans, strategies, and justification. It provides a baseline for assessing progress.
Project log
(Daily log)		 Dynamic Created in SU by the PM. It tracks current tasks, informal problems, and risks before they enter the official registers.
