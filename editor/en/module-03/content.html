Chapter 3: People

Source: Managing Successful Projects with PRINCE2, chapter 3.

The atmosphere in the SnakeBite team is becoming tense. Developer Bartek does not want to work with designer Tomek, claiming that the retro style limits his coding ambitions. Maya, meanwhile, is afraid that the team will not understand the nostalgic atmosphere of earlier years.

Zosia knows that a Gantt chart alone will not solve the relationship problems. She must apply leadership, take care of team culture, ensure effective communication, and guide people through change management.
