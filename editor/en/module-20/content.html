Chapter 20: Foundation review

Purpose, decisions and SnakeBite scenario

PRINCE2 7 has five integrated elements: principles, people, practices, processes and the project context. The seven principles are continued business justification; learn from experience; define roles, responsibilities and relationships; manage by stages; manage by exception; focus on products; and tailor to suit the project. They guide the way the method is applied to the project.

SnakeBite recap: Zosia and Artur revisit the business case at stage boundaries. Maya states user needs; Kamil represents the supplier; Tomek delivers agreed work packages. The board authorizes stages, responds to stage exceptions and decides on closure. Acceptance of the game is an output decision; users’ adoption and later benefits still need measurement.

Key concepts and management products

Seven practices: business case, organizing, plans, quality, risk, issues and progress. Seven processes: starting up a project, directing a project, initiating a project, controlling a stage, managing product delivery, managing a stage boundary and closing a project. Processes are not seven sequential management stages. Seven performance targets: benefits, cost, time, quality, scope, risk and sustainability. A forecast breach of delegated tolerance triggers escalation; a current deviation may remain within tolerance.

Name the five integrated elements.

Principles, people, practices, processes and project context.

How many PRINCE2 principles are there?

Seven, all of which must be applied.

What are the seven performance targets?

Benefits, cost, time, quality, scope, risk and sustainability.

Are seven processes seven project stages?

No; stages are management control periods, while processes describe project activities.

What triggers exception escalation?

A forecast breach of the tolerance delegated to a management level.

Does product acceptance establish all benefits?

No; benefits can arise and be measured after closure.

Additional textbook-aligned explanation

Connect the method

PRINCE2 brings together five integrated elements: principles, people, practices, processes and project context. The seven principles guide decisions; seven practices inform management; seven processes describe activities across the project life cycle. Process names do not imply seven sequential management stages. The project board directs by authorization and exception; the project manager controls the stage; a team manager delivers agreed work packages.

Revise through decisions

Begin with continued business justification, define products and responsibilities, set tolerances for seven performance targets and report forecasts at the appropriate level. Record risks as uncertain events and issues as matters needing decisions. At boundaries reassess the business case and plan the next stage. At closure obtain formal acceptance, transfer outstanding actions and arrange after-project benefits measurement.
